YORK, Pa. (WHTM) — BAE Systems, which has a facility in York County, recently received a contract from the U.S. Army that is valued at more than $500 million.

According to BAE Systems, this new contract is for the production of additional M109A7 Paladin Self-Propelled Howitzers and M992A3 Ammunition Carriers for the U.S. Army.

“The M109A7 Paladin Self-Propelled Howitzer is designed to provide a significant operational advantage on today’s battlefields and into the future,” Dan Furber, program director for Artillery and Combat Support for the Combat Mission Systems business at BAE Systems said. “Its proven performance demonstrates its value on the field, and we’re proud to support the U.S. Army and its allies in leveraging this critical capability.”

BAE Systems says the contract, which was awarded in December 2025, will bolster the Army’s ability to meet critical operational requirements, particularly for its Armored Brigade Combat Teams (ABCTs).

BAE Systems received a contract award valued at more than $500 million to produce additional M109A7 Paladin Self-Propelled Howitzers and M992A3 Ammunition Carriers for the U.S. Army. (Credit: BAE Systems)

The company notes that although this is one of the lightest tracked self-propelled howitzer systems on the battlefield, the vehicle is engineered with an adaptable chassis and next-gen capabilities.

Additionally, the platform integrates cutting-edge technology and a modern architecture that incorporates advanced digital fire control systems, known as the “digital backbone.”

According to BAE Systems, these capabilities provide a boost in lethality, survivability, and responsiveness.

The M109A7 is being produced in York, Pa.; Elgin, OK; and Anniston, AL.
