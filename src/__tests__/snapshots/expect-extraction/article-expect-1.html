YORK COUNTY, Pa. (WHTM) — Crews are battling a huge fire at a York County salvage yard on Wednesday afternoon.

It was just before 2 p.m. when first responders got a call about a building fire at the J&K Salvage off of Kings Road in Spring Garden Township, a spokesperson for York County Office of Emergency Management (OEM) said. Upon their arrival, crews saw there were also cars and tractor-trailers on fire.


Smoke can be seen filling the sky for miles coming from J&K Salvage off of Kings Road in Spring Garden Township as first responders fight a fire, photos posted on Facebook by Greater York Professional Fire Fighters show.

A HAZMAT team is also on site, the OEM spokesperson said.

The public is being asked to avoid the area.
