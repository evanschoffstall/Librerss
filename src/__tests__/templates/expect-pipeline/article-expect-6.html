(WHTM) — U.S. Senator Dave McCormick reacted to the United States and Israel’s attack on Iran Saturday morning.

The attack, according to the Associated Press, appeared to hit the offices of Iran’s Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei in Tehran this morning after President Donald Trump told the Iranian people to “seize control of your destiny” by rising against its leadership. The AP says Iran fired missiles and drones toward Israel and U.S. military bases in the region in retaliation.

Senator McCormick (R) released a response to the attack on Facebook, saying President Trump gave the Iranian regime “a chance for a deal,” but they “rejected a path to peace and prosperity.”

“I am praying for our brave troops and our steadfast allies who stand with us during this challenging and noble mission,” McCormick said.

“Now would be a good time for Democrats to drop their opposition to DHS funding and pass the bill to support our homeland security,” McCormick said in another statement on X. “Continuing to play political games with our national security given the unfolding situation in the Middle East is dangerous.”

President Trump has claimed that Iran’s nuclear program has continued developing, and they have created missiles capable of reaching the United States.

State Rep. Scott Perry (R-10) also reacted to the attack on social media, telling the Iranian public, “The hour of your freedom has arrived. Your destiny is in your hands. TAKE BACK YOUR GOVERNMENT, AND RETURN PERSIA.”

U.S. Senator John Fetterman (D-PA) also released a statement, saying, “God bless the United States, our great military, and Israel.”
